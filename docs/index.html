A platform game through five haunted places, from the graveyard to the mansion, screen by screen by ladders and doors: the hero jumps, throws his weapon at the yōkai, picks up what he finds and in each place beats the event that guards it. The title comes from running the ROM, checked against openMSX point by point; the game in GIF, played in openMSX; the tunes, from its own engine, identical to openMSX frame by frame in those that play in the recordings.

Casio - (c) 1986 - 32 KB cartridge (header at 0x4000) - MSX - Dump 15345c87...

There are no screenshots of the game here. The title comes from running the ROM and the GIFs are the ROM running in openMSX: on every frame the VRAM, the RAM and the VDP registers are logged, and our own code paints them.

What is inside

The game

  • The game: the CASIO intro, the title, the controls, the lives and the endurance, the falls and GAME OVER.
  • The places: the five places and their 74 screens, the events and the ending.
  • The texts: every text in the ROM.
  • The music: the tunes, from its own sound engine and checked against openMSX.

Curiosities

  • Curiosities: the five keys on the title, F1, a demo that plays the BIOS, leftovers.

Under the hood

  • Under the hood: rebuilding the ROM, the cartridge, the BIOS it uses, the loop, the screens, the RAM and the tools.
